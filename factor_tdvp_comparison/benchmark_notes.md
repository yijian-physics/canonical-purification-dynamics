# Reference and fairness audit

This note records the comparison framework audit, separate from measured results.

- **A/B channel convention:** local jump `sqrt(gamma_j/2) Z_j` gives coherence `exp(-gamma_j t)` and flip probability `(1-exp(-gamma_j t))/2`. Every diagonal controlled-phase circuit commutes with these jumps, so the exact density/root are obtained by conjugating products of one-site densities/roots. This works for non-Clifford angles as well as cluster CZ gates. Site and bond arrays in the existing drivers are zero-indexed.
- **D preparation:** `prepared_power` implements X-polarized product, nearest-neighbor controlled phases, then all-site Hadamards. Its onsite channel has coherence `exp(-t)`. The nine-test framework suite independently compares dense initial and evolved physical densities against this MPO provider.
- **E reference:** the transverse coefficient alone decays as `0.4 exp(-t)`. The parity term remains constant. Analytic future roots are valid scoring inputs, not factor-TDVP evolution targets.
- **C endpoint equivalence:** `gamma*T=-log(1-2p)` for the generator `gamma(P rho P-rho)/2`. This equality is directly checked against a dense Lindblad exponential for all four requested tilts. A finite-channel local lift may use one update while TDVP takes multiple substeps; the physical endpoint is what must match.
- **C information budgets:** the practical three-site unitary is trained on five-site physical data. The three-site half-Petz construction uses its three-site active region. Equal active support does not imply equal information/preparation costs. The exact-target optimum is a validation lower bound over gates, not a deployable baseline that avoids the exact future root.
- **F critical historical values:** the reported `4.92e-6` and `3.10e-7` are corrected-root trajectory/endpoint results using dense physical-density correction; they are not ordinary uncorrected TDVP errors. `critical_late.csv` also resolves them as representation-floor values when update bond reaches 256. The matching eight-step corrected trajectory is in `cp_accuracy_refinement.trajectory` and should retain its separate label and correction cost.
- **F rank:** a zero instantaneous kernel-leakage block does not rule out delayed dissipative opening. A finite channel bridge, followed by support audit, is required before treating the factor derivative as smooth. Physical rank and MPO bond rank are distinct diagnostics.
- **G local quantities:** historical `local_cp_large` columns use trace distance `0.5||a-b||_1` on a specified doubled two-site marginal, often relative to a finite-window reference. They are neither global Frobenius root errors nor automatically certified local errors. If reproduced, retain the original norm/region labels; a separate Frobenius number can be added but must not silently replace the historical metric.
- **Metric policy:** a general output amplitude A represents density `A A†`. Its global canonical-root error is `||A-sqrt(rho_exact)||F` with no ancilla-unitary optimization and no silent renormalization. The factor method supplies `A=HH†`. The separate branch defect is `||A-sqrt(A A†)||F`; evaluating the latter root through A's SVD avoids density-level roundoff in a known kernel.
- **Certificate policy:** the witness output bound is a mathematical bound evaluated in floating point under a physical inverse-metric assumption. It must be shown distinctly from an independently measured global error. Neither local marginal agreement nor solver stationarity is a global-error certificate.
- **Timing policy:** establish a common end-to-end timing including initial physical data and setup; report evolution-only timing separately. Where an old driver does not expose a split, retain a combined timing and state that limitation. Scoring references can cost more than evolution and should be separated when exposed. Parallel executions provide no reliable isolated machine-to-machine speed benchmark.

## Audit of repeated cluster local-unitary fitting

`audit_local_unitary.py` isolates the first left-boundary update of the four-spin cluster benchmark. At active width two and fitting width three, the unchanged regularized fit has local residual `5.77e-13` but actual global root error `0.102259760696`. Its physical marginal input agrees with an independent direct partial trace to `1.45e-16`; applying the fitted unitary densely agrees with the adapter's tensor application to `8.0e-16`. The retained bond cap is 64 and truncation is negligible. These checks exclude a cache, reshape, gate-placement, or bond-cap explanation for the observed first error.

The source rows from the local canonical purification span a two-dimensional active subspace, while the true global canonical purification has active components outside it with total norm `1/sqrt(2)`. Enlarging the fitting region to the whole four-spin chain restores the required four-dimensional span and reduces global error to `1.15e-12`. The same-support exact-target unitary optimum has error about `1.2e-15`. Thus a near-zero local fit residual does not determine the unitary action needed by the actual global purification. This is a limitation of the tested finite-training local-root fitting prescription on this input, not proof that no accurate local unitary exists. The unregularized adapter and bond caps 4/64 exhibit the same first-update error in the independent investigation; the saved audit focuses on the unchanged regularized construction and a full-fitting control.

The audit is now a regression test: it checks accurate physical data and gate application, the discrepancy between local and global objectives, and restoration by the larger fitting region. It does not alter the baseline fitting algorithm to improve its score.
